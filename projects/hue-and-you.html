Hue & You

Branding & Social Media

Hue & You is a coloring book brand centered around creativity, mindfulness, and finding joy in everyday moments. The visual identity combines playful typography, soft purple tones, organic shapes, and an expressive mascot to create a welcoming and approachable personality.

The brand identity extends across social media graphics, digital wallpapers, illustrated merchandise, and Etsy product listings. Each design reinforces a consistent visual identity while encouraging people of all ages to slow down, unwind, and enjoy coloring.

Project scope
Visual Identity, Mascot Design, Illustration, Social Media Design, Digital Marketing Graphics & Merchandise Design.
