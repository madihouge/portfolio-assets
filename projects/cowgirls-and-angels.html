Cowgirls & Angels

Cowgirls & Angels is a Western-inspired boutique packaging project that combines rustic charm with feminine elegance. The design draws inspiration from traditional Western details, warm desert tones, delicate botanical illustrations, and vintage typography.

The packaging collection features a warm palette of cream, terracotta, and deep brown, paired with illustrated cowboy boots, stars, and floral elements. Each piece is designed to create a cohesive and thoughtful shopping and unboxing experience that feels personal, welcoming, and timeless.
