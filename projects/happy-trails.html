Happy Trails

2024

Happy Trails is an outdoor goods brand inspired by the spirit of adventure, the beauty of nature, and the simple joys of exploring the outdoors.

The visual identity combines earthy colors, vintage-inspired typography, topographic patterns, and playful character design to create a brand that feels warm, approachable, and adventurous.

At the heart of the identity is Benny, a lovable bear mascot who represents curiosity, exploration, and a love for the outdoors. His personality comes to life through different poses and everyday outdoor adventures.

The branding extends across apparel, drinkware, stickers, embroidered patches, and printed materials, creating a cohesive identity that captures the nostalgic charm of outdoor exploration.
