Love Mads

Love Mads is a stationery brand inspired by handwritten letters, thoughtful gestures, and the little joys of everyday life. The visual identity captures the warmth and personal connection of receiving something made with love.

The brand combines soft blue tones, warm cream, delicate handwritten typography, playful hearts, and floral illustrations to create a nostalgic yet timeless identity. These elements are carried throughout the stationery collection, creating a cohesive visual experience that feels charming, approachable, and personal.
