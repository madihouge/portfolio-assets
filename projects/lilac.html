Lilac Beauty

Packaging Design

Lilac Beauty is a beauty and body care packaging concept inspired by femininity, botanical details, and the everyday ritual of self-care. The design combines a soft lavender color palette, delicate floral illustrations, elegant serif typography, and flowing script lettering to create a refined yet approachable aesthetic.

The packaging system carries these elements across retail bags, gift boxes, tissue paper, and body care products, creating a cohesive experience from the storefront to the unboxing. Each detail is designed to reflect softness, elegance, and the brand's message of self-worth.
